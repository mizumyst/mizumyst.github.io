Selected Interests

Puzzlehunts: Collections of puzzles characterized by solving a wide variety of puzzles to progress through a competition or story. Puzzlehunt puzzles may resemble crosswords, logic puzzles, and other familiar genres, but usually require deriving a word or phrase without explicit instructions. I help with EC Puzzle Hunt. The Puzzle Wiki is a great community resource for learning about puzzle hunts and common puzzles within them.

Vocaloid: A singing synthesis software, but when genericized, a "genre" of music characterized by synthesized vocals, often in Japanese. Most of my favourite music belongs here, and here are some of my current recommendations (it's so hard to provide a condensed list, and I'll definitely talk more about this elsewhere).

Conlangs: Short for constructed languages, these languages are intentionally designed rather than occuring naturally among a certain population. I have a conlang/worldbuilding project currently in the works, and when enough initial details are finalized I'll share it somewhere.
